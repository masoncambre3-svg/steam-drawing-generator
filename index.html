Steam Drawing Generator

Lay out traps, strainers, valves, manifolds, equipment, fittings and pipe at true scale, then copy the drawing into a report.

Selection

Add a product

Select a few items (Shift+drag a box), name them and save them as an assembly. They show here, ready to add to any drawing.

Pipe

Click on the sheet to start a pipe, click again for each turn, and double-click to finish. Runs lock to 45° steps.

After the first click, aim with the mouse, type a length and press Enter. Bare numbers are inches. Enter on an empty box finishes.

Text

Click on the sheet where the label should go.

Arrow

Click where the arrow starts, then where it points.

Dimension

Click two points, then click where the dimension line goes. Points snap to product connections and pipe ends and turns.

      Bill of materials

      Include

      Counted from the drawing: one line per model, size and connection. Pipe is the centreline length, rounded up to the next foot. Bends drawn with the pipe tool are not counted.
